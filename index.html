Our Reasoning

Why We Focus on Finding It Early

1

It starts earlier here

South Asians have 2 to 5 times the risk of type 2 diabetes compared with white Europeans, and get it 5 to 10 years earlier, often at a normal weight. In India, 62% of daily calories come from carbohydrates, mostly white rice in the South.

Sources: Diabetologia 2022; ICMR-INDIAB, Nature Medicine 2025

2

It hides until it hurts

Nearly 39 million Indians have diabetes and don't know it. In one rural Tamil Nadu screening project, among people with diabetes who were checked for complications, 18% already had eye damage and 31% had nerve damage.

Sources: IDF Atlas 2025; Chunampet project, J Diabetes Sci Technol 2012

3

Finding it early helps

In a Tamil Nadu village, a 7-month health education program lowered fasting blood sugar by 11% in adults with prediabetes and 25% in adults with diabetes. People can only make those changes once they know their numbers.

Source: Diabetes Care 2008

The Gap We Fill

What Stands in the Way, and What We Do

Cost

One A1C test costs more than a day's rural wage, and 38% of Indian households with diabetes face catastrophic health costs.

Our screening camps are free.

Access

A1C testing is rarely available at rural primary health centres, so a test can mean a trip to the city.

We bring a lab partner to the village.

Awareness

Without a test, high blood sugar has few early signs, so people don't know to see a doctor.

Everyone gets their own result and is encouraged to follow up with a doctor.

93%

At our second camp, 69 of the 74 people tested had an A1C in the prediabetes or diabetes range.

This is why we keep going.

Our Approach

The Nalam Model

A three-pillar approach to shift the health paradigm from late-stage treatment to early-stage prevention.

Free A1C Testing

Partnering with vetted local laboratories to provide gold-standard blood sugar testing at no cost to the community.

Learn more

Targeted Education

Bilingual Tamil/English guidance on nutrition, local foods to eat and avoid, and exercise routines tailored to the community.

Learn more

Data-Driven Follow-up

WhatsApp-based tracking to monitor high-risk individuals and send timely reminders for 3 or 6-month re-testing.

Learn more

Research & Reading

Understanding the Crisis

Key research and reports that inform our mission and the urgency of diabetes prevention in India.

The Lancet

ICMR National Diabetes Survey Across India

India's largest study estimating 101 million people with diabetes and 136 million with prediabetes.

IDF Atlas

IDF Diabetes Atlas: India Profile

Official data on India's diabetes burden with projections showing a 75% increase by 2050.

The Lancet / WHO

India's 212 Million Diabetes Cases

WHO-backed global study estimating a quarter of the world's diabetes cases are in India. It counts more people than IDF because it uses a broader test definition.

The Lancet

Public Health Systems for Diabetes in South Asia

Reviews community health worker strategies for type 2 diabetes prevention in South Asia.

PMC / Diabetes Science

Telemedicine Diabetes Prevention in Rural Tamil Nadu

Reports on the Chunampet project that screened 86.5% of adults across 42 Tamil Nadu villages.

Diabetes Care / ADA

Community Prevention Program in Rural Tamil Nadu

Village-level lifestyle intervention that reduced fasting glucose by 11-25% in participants.
