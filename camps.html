Free A1C Screening Camps

Since launching in May 2026, we've run two free A1C screening camps in Coimbatore and tested 114 people. A screening camp is a free community event where anyone can get an A1C test and advice on what their result means. Each camp has its own page with what we found and what happened next.

Privacy

How We Report Results

  • We only share totals, never anyone's individual result.
  • Groups smaller than 10 people are shown as "fewer than 10."
  • A screening test isn't a diagnosis, so we report A1C ranges: normal (below 5.7%), prediabetes range (5.7–6.4%) and diabetes range (6.5% or higher).
